# Onboarding Question Bank

Draw from these during `onboarding/flow.md`. Not a script to read verbatim — pick what's
relevant, ask conversationally, and skip what doesn't apply. **One group's worth per turn** (`flow.md`
→ "One topic per turn"), and let a listed entry's details come from the answer rather than asking
them part by part.

**Uploads welcome for any section.** A screenshot or document (org chart, planning doc, team
roster, calendar) is often better than typed answers — extract the details, write the memory,
and confirm with the principal. See each step's "Best upload" in `flow.md`.

**Speaking is welcome too** — the principal can dictate or talk their answers instead of typing;
encourage it, especially for the longer sections.

## Pick the track first (see `flow.md` → Modes)
- **Work or personal?** Infer from the opening; ask one line only if unclear.
- **(Work) IC or manager?** Infer from the role/title; ask only if ambiguous.
- The **Work track** is the sections immediately below (use the **Manager**/**IC** emphasis notes in
  People and Priorities). For a **personal** Chief of Staff, skip them and use the **Personal track** at the end.

# Work track

## The principal (→ profile)
- What's your name and role/title? **And what should I call you?** (record what they go by — I'll use
  it naturally in conversation).
- What organization are you part of, and how big is your team/scope?
- What are you ultimately responsible for?
- What does success look like for you over the next quarter? The next year?

## Organization & context (→ context)
- How is your team/org structured? (names come at People, below)
- What does the company/group do, in one or two lines?
- Any acronyms, product names, or jargon I should know?

## Tools & connectors (→ `connector-check`, `connector-recommend`)
- What tools and systems do you live in day to day? (calendar, email, docs, PM/task, dev, chat —
  so I can recommend which **Connectors** to enable; see `brain/integrations/connectors.md`)
- Google or Microsoft for calendar/email? (→ Google Calendar/Gmail, or the Microsoft 365 connector)

## People (→ people, one file each — walk every circle)
*Emphasis by role: **manager** — lean into direct reports, skip-down, and cross-org stakeholders (the
team they run). **IC** — lean into their manager, skip-up, and the close peers/collaborators they
depend on; don't dwell on a downward org they don't have.*
- **Manager (up):** Who do you report to?
- **Skip-level up:** Who's your manager's manager — and how involved are they?
- **Peers:** Who's at your level that you work with most — especially cross-functional peers you
  depend on?
- **Direct reports:** Who's on your team?
- **Skip-level down:** Beneath your directs, who are the key people you should know?
- **Cross-org:** Who else across the company matters to you — other functions, exec sponsors,
  partner teams — even with no reporting line?
- **External:** Who are your key external stakeholders — customers, partners, investors, board?
- For **each** person: their role, your relationship, how often you meet, and anything about how to
  work with them (preferences, sensitivities, what they care about). Tag their `relationship:`.
- Who are the stakeholders on your most important initiatives?

## Priorities & projects (→ projects, one file each)
*Emphasis by role: **manager** — the team's initiatives, OKRs, and the RAPID decisions they own.
**IC** — their own deliverables and goals; OKRs optional, RAPID only if they actually drive decisions.*
- What are your top 3–5 priorities right now?
- For each initiative: what's the goal, who owns it, what's the status, and what are the key
  dates?
- What's at risk or blocked?
- **Do you run on OKRs?** What are your objectives and key results this quarter/year? (→ `okrs.md`;
  I'll record them and can pressure-test the KRs — see `brain/playbooks/okrs.md`.)
- **What big decisions are you weighing right now?** Who recommends, who has to agree, who decides?
  (→ `decisions.md`, tracked with RAPID roles — see `brain/playbooks/decisions.md`.)

## Working preferences (→ preferences)
- How do you like your answers — length, level of detail? *(Don't ask how to reach you — I'm always
  here in the desktop app; just start a session in this folder and I load your context.)*
- How do you like to be briefed before meetings and decisions?
- What are your standing rules or hard nos (calendar, interruptions, escalation)?
- How proactive do you want me to be — draft-and-wait, or act-and-inform?

## Routines & cadence
- Do you want scheduled briefs (daily / weekly retro / weekly preview) set up as **routines**?
- What are your recurring rhythms (weekly review, 1:1s, reporting)?
- Which of my playbooks matter most to you (weekly review, meeting prep, decision briefs,
  inbox triage)?
- Are there recurring commitments I should always track?

# Personal track

Use this instead of the work sections when `Context: personal`. Same six steps; life, not org. No
OKRs, no RAPID, no reporting lines.

## You & household (→ profile)
- What's your name, and what should I call you?
- Who's in your household / immediate family I should know about?
- What's the shape of your life right now — the big things on your plate?
- What would a good few months look like for you personally?

## Life context (→ context, one file per topic)
- Home & household logistics (where you live, key routines, anything I should hold).
- Finances at a high level (accounts/advisors to remember — not sensitive numbers unless you want).
- Health & family logistics (appointments, care you coordinate).
- Travel patterns and preferences.

## Tools & connectors (→ `connector-check`, `connector-recommend`)
- Which of your tools should I lean on — **calendar and email** first (Google or Microsoft)?

## Key people (→ people, one file each — natural relationship labels)
- **Family** — partner, kids, parents, others you coordinate with (`relationship: family`).
- **Close friends** you rely on or plan with (`relationship: friend`).
- **Advisors / providers** — doctor, accountant, lawyer, contractor, agent (`relationship: advisor`
  or `provider`).
- For each: how you're connected, how often you're in touch, anything useful about working with them.

## Goals & plans (→ `projects/`, one file each)
- What are you trying to get done — a move, a trip, a renovation, a health goal, a course, a side
  interest?
- For each: what "done" looks like, key dates, who's involved, what's blocked.
- Any recurring commitments I should always track (→ `memory/commitments.md`)?

## Preferences (→ preferences)
- How do you like your answers — length, tone?
- Standing rules / hard nos (quiet hours, what to never surface, how proactive to be).

## Routines & cadence
- Recurring rhythms — a weekly review, bills/renewals, standing family or household commitments.
- Want scheduled briefs (a daily look-ahead, a weekly review) set up as routines?
