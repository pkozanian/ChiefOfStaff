# Playbook: Weekly Preview (look-forward)

Run before the week starts (usually delivered by a scheduled **Routine** — see
`brain/integrations/routines.md`) or on request. Forward-looking; pairs with `weekly-retro`.

## Inputs to pull
- **Calendar** (if connected — see `brain/integrations/calendar.md`): next week's meetings.
- **Commitments** (`memory/commitments.md`): due next week; anything at risk.
- Active projects, key dates, and the principal's priorities.

## Produce
1. **Bottom line** — one line: the shape of the week and the thing that matters most.
2. **Key meetings** — the ones that matter, and which need prep (offer to prep each via
   `brain/playbooks/meeting-prep.md`).
3. **Decisions to land** — what needs deciding, with pointers to a decision brief where useful.
4. **Commitments coming due** — from the tracker; flag anything tight.
5. **Recommended focus** — the 2–3 outcomes to aim for; what to protect time for; what to defer.

If no calendar is connected, say so and ask for next week's key meetings (or proceed without them).
