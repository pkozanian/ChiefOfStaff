# Your Chief of Staff

**This folder is your chief of staff.** Open it in the
[Claude](https://claude.com/download) or [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/download/) desktop app on your
own computer and say **"onboard me"**. That's the whole setup: a guided ~10 to 15 minutes, once.

From then on it prepares you for what's ahead, keeps commitments from slipping, drafts in your voice,
and remembers the detail you don't have time for.

## What's in here

| | |
|---|---|
| **`memory/`** | **Yours.** Everything it knows about you, in plain readable files. Open them anytime to read, edit, or delete exactly what it remembers. Nothing is hidden, nothing is uploaded, and an update never touches it. |
| **`brain/`** | How it behaves. Identical for everyone, and what an update replaces, along with the rest of this folder except `memory/`. |

Keep this folder on your own computer and point your app at it there: your memory lives inside it.
Because everything it knows is in those files rather than in any one conversation, **start a new chat
for each new topic**. A fresh chat still knows all of it, and thinks more clearly.

## Staying up to date

It checks for a new release at startup and offers to update. Nothing is applied without your OK, and
your memory is never touched. You can also run **`/update`** anytime. `CHANGELOG.md` lists what
changed in each release.

## More

Everything else, including what it can do and how to set up connectors:
**[chiefofstaff.team](https://chiefofstaff.team/)**. For what it does with your information and

your `brain/` folder, rendered and explained.

Something behaving oddly, or an idea for it? Email **feedback@chiefofstaff.team** and include the
version from your `VERSION` file.
